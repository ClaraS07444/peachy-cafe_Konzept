About peachy

Made for good days.

A café concept built around thoughtful coffee, colourful food and a little more room to slow down.

Our story · Concept

A bright idea for everyday moments.

peachy began as a simple concept: bring coffee, good food and sunny everyday moments together in a place that feels calm, warm and easy to return to.

This is an independent C Agency portfolio concept. The story demonstrates the intended personality of the brand rather than the history of a real café.

What we believe

Simple things, done with care.

02
